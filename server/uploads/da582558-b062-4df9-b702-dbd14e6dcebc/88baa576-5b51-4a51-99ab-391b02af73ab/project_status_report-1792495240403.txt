PROJECT PHOENIX - MOBILE APP REDESIGN
Q3 STATUS REPORT | Reporting period: 1 July - 30 September 2026
Prepared by: Ravi Menon (Project Manager) | Sponsor: Anita Desai (VP Product)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Project Phoenix is rebuilding the customer mobile app (Android and iOS) with a new design, faster checkout and in-app support chat. The project is 68% complete and currently AMBER: the scope is on track, but the launch date is at risk because of a delay in the payment gateway integration.
Planned launch: 15 December 2026. Current forecast: 8 January 2027 (24 days late) unless the risks in Section 4 are resolved.
